Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Crestify ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our social media analytics dashboard.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with TikTok, we collect the following information through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Profile Information: Your display name, username, avatar, and bio (via user.info.basic and user.info.profile)
  • Account Statistics: Follower count, following count, likes count, and video count (via user.info.stats)
  • Video Data: Your public video information including view counts, likes, comments, and shares (via video.list)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to provide you with analytics and insights about your TikTok account performance. This includes generating charts, tracking growth over time, and identifying content trends. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or TikTok data with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share data only when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We retain your TikTok data only for as long as your account is active. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. TikTok Data

Our use of TikTok data is governed by TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and API policies. We access your TikTok data only with your explicit consent through TikTok's Login Kit authorization flow. You can revoke our access at any time through your TikTok account settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may disconnect your TikTok account from Crestify, which will remove your data from our systems. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@crestify.app.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
