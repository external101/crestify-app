Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Crestify and how it works.

How does Crestify connect to my TikTok account?
Crestify uses TikTok's official Login Kit for secure authentication. You sign in with your TikTok credentials on TikTok's own page, and we receive authorized access to your profile and analytics data. We never see or store your password.
What data does Crestify access?
We access your basic profile information (display name, avatar, bio), follower and engagement statistics (follower count, likes, video count), and your public video list. This powers the analytics dashboards you see in your account. We do not post content or modify your account.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data to third parties and only use it to generate your personal analytics. You can disconnect your account and delete all stored data at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I try Crestify before paying?
Absolutely. Our Free plan is fully functional for one TikTok account with 7 days of data history. There is no credit card required. You can upgrade to Pro at any time to unlock unlimited history and advanced features.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your Pro plan from your account settings at any time. Your account will continue on the Free plan at the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
